## 10 Manufacturing Capabilities & Services
1. **Jewellery Manufacturing**: Full-cycle precious metal casting and fabrication.
2. **Jewellery Buffing**: Primary abrasive compound wheel deburring and seam removal.
3. **Jewellery Polishing**: Multi-stage Tripoli and fine rouge mirror polishing.
4. **Surface Finishing**: Satin wire-brushing, bead-blasting, matte frosting, and texturing.
5. **Product Refinishing**: Scratch removal, re-burnishing, and pre-owned jewelry renewal.
6. **Jewellery Processing**: Ultrasonic cavitation, chemical degreasing, and tumbling.
7. **Product Assembly**: Micro-torch/laser soldering, linking, clasp fitment, and setting prep.
8. **Custom Manufacturing**: Confidential OEM/ODM custom batch contract production.
9. **Quality Inspection**: Systematic 10x optical loupe and microscopic parameter audits.
10. **Final Product Finishing**: Steam decontamination, anti-tarnish passivation, and sealed B2B packaging.
