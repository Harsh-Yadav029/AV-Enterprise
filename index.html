Manufacturing Capabilities 10 Dedicated Production Services

From initial casting ingestion to final sealed export packaging, A V Enterprises executes end-to-end manufacturing and finishing operations with verified quality controls at every station.

01 Fabrication

Jewellery Manufacturing

Full-cycle jewellery production translating CAD blueprints and master master-molds into precious metal forms in gold, silver, and alloy metals with strict metallurgical integrity.

02 Abrasive Prep

Jewellery Buffing

Primary abrasive compound wheel processing to remove parting lines, casting skin, sprue stubs, and microscopic porosity while maintaining crisp edge geometries and stone prong integrity.

03 High Gloss

Jewellery Polishing

Multi-stage Tripoli cut-down and ultra-fine rouge polishing on unstitched canton flannel wheels, yielding radiant mirror luster and distortion-free optical reflectivity.

04 Texturing

06 Chemical Prep

Jewellery Processing

Chemical degreasing, ultrasonic cavitation baths, pickling, magnetic pin tumbling, and thermal annealing cycles preparing raw and intermediate pieces for subsequent fabrication stages.

Manufacturing Processes Five Calibrated Production Stages

Every production run follows a standardized 5-stage sequential engineering methodology designed for absolute batch consistency, surface clarity, and structural longevity.

  1. 1

    Cast & Ingest

    Initial casting inspection, sprue cut-off, weight verification, and dimensional sorting in our clean preparation bay.

  2. 2

    Pre-Buff & Prep

    Coarse abrasive deburring on sisal and stitched muslin wheels to eliminate casting skins, joint lines, and micro-imperfections.

  3. 3

    Polish & Texture

    Staged Tripoli and rouge polishing, followed by client-specified surface textures (satin, sandblast, or mirror shine).

  4. 4

    Stone & Assemble

    Precision component joining, clasp fitment, prong tightening, and structural checking under magnifying loupes.

  5. 5

    Inspect & Finish

    Multi-tank ultrasonic cleaning, high-pressure steam blast, anti-tarnish protective sealing, and final lot packaging.

Buffing & Polishing The Benchmark of Surface Quality

As Delhi's dedicated jewellery finishing specialists, A V Enterprises treats buffing and polishing as an exact science. Our controlled wheel speeds, compound selections, and pressure vectors eliminate surface distortion while delivering exceptional luster.

What manufacturing & finishing services does A V Enterprises provide?

We specialize in 10 core manufacturing capabilities: full jewellery manufacturing, high-precision buffing, staged rouge polishing, surface texturing (satin, bead-blast, matte), product refinishing, chemical processing, multi-part assembly, custom OEM/ODM contract manufacturing, microscopic quality inspection, and final product packaging.

What is the business association between A V Enterprises and Ashen Craft?

A V Enterprises serves as the dedicated manufacturing and product-finishing partner for relevant Ashen Craft products. Jewellery and associated merchandise supplied under the Ashen Craft brand undergo applicable manufacturing, processing, buffing, and polishing operations through A V Enterprises. Ashen Craft operates as the customer-facing brand, while A V Enterprises represents the manufacturing partner. Both entities maintain separate and distinct business identities.

What production volumes and batch sizes can your Delhi facility handle?

Our infrastructure accommodates both specialized pilot prototype runs (sample development) and scalable high-throughput commercial batches (hundreds to thousands of units) with synchronized delivery schedules and consistent finishing standards across every lot.

How does A V Enterprises protect brand design confidentiality?

We execute formal non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) for all OEM/ODM partners. CAD files, silicone master molds, sample prototypes, and finished inventory are stored under strict access-controlled protocols at our facility.

Can we schedule a factory visit or submit samples for buffing and polishing evaluation?

Yes. B2B clients and brand partners are welcome to visit our facility at 24 Civil Lines, Shri Ram Road, Delhi by scheduled appointment. We also accept test castings or master samples for comparative finishing audits and quotation.
